PSDS Phase 3a: historical record

Phase 3a was the first application window of PSDS Phase 3, announced at £1.425 billion over 2022/23 to 2024/25. It opened on 6 October 2021 and closed on 3 November 2021, and eligible applications were assessed in the order they were received [DESNZ].

Budget and timing

Phase 3 had a budget of £475 million for each financial year. In 2022/23, 85% (£403.75 million) was for single-year projects and 15% (£71.25 million) for the first year of multi-year projects. Every project had to start delivery in 2022/23; single-year projects had to finish by 31 March 2023 [DESNZ].

Applications and awards

  • 381 applications asking for £861.4 million in total.
  • 231 projects awarded over £613 million, delivered by 170 public sector organisations.
  • Schools and academies: 62 grants (26% of grants) worth £42.7 million (7% of value). The NHS took £360.7 million (59%).

All figures from the DESNZ Phase 3a summary report [DESNZ]. The full list of recipients is in the Phase 3 publication.

£/tCO2e test

Phase 3a used the Carbon Cost Threshold of £325 of grant per tonne of direct carbon saved over the lifetime of the measures, the level set in Phase 2 [DESNZ]. Phase 4 later replaced it with a £510/tCO2e Grant Carbon Cost ceiling (see the ceiling explainer).

What changed next

Phase 3b kept the 3a eligibility rules and added planning-year applications and sector soft caps, both in response to feedback and monitoring of earlier phases. See Phase 3b and the full timeline.
