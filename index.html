Public sector decarbonisation scheme · schools

The PSC scheme for schools, without the marketing copy.

The Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme (PSDS) was the main capital grant route at school scale for heat-pump and fabric retrofit. It is closed to new applications, and the government has confirmed no further PSDS investment beyond currently-awarded projects. This site answers the eligibility question in 30 seconds, costs your scheme against the £510/tCO2e Phase 4 Grant Carbon Cost ceiling, and routes you to the live alt-funding programmes that have replaced it.

Direct answer · 10 August 2026

PSDS Phase 4 closed for applications November 2024 [Salix]. At the June 2025 Spending Review the government confirmed no further PSDS investment beyond currently-awarded projects [Salix]; awarded projects stay funded (Phase 3c delivery to 31 March 2026, Phase 4 to 31 March 2028) but no Phase 5 has been announced. The most recent binding rule was Phase 4's £510/tCO2e Grant Carbon Cost ceiling, with a minimum 12% applicant contribution for the like-for-like counterfactual. State-funded schools displacing gas, oil or LPG heating on owned estate were in scope; independent schools and PFI sites without landlord consent were not. Live alternatives for schools right now: DfE Renewal & Retrofit (£710m, April 2026 [DfE]), CIF for academies, GB Energy schools solar, Warm Homes Plan.

Three outcomes. One lane each.

OUTCOME 1
Would have qualified for PSDS

State school, gas heating, owned estate. PSDS is closed to new applications, so run the £510/tCO2e check to understand your scheme, then take the pre-feasibility pack to a live route like DfE Renewal & Retrofit.

OUTCOME 2
Eligible · but PSDS has closed

No further PSDS funding is coming. The DfE £710m R&R, CIF, GB Energy solar and Warm Homes Plan are all live. Pivot the bid pack to the right route now.

OUTCOME 3
Out of scope for PSDS

Independent, PFI without consent, or above the GCC ceiling. PPA, ESCO, Re:Fit and commercial heat-pump finance are the routes. We explain each plainly.
