Answer all three to see the outcome. Three questions, roughly 30 seconds.

What this tool decides

The wizard encodes the three binding gates in the published PSDS grant conditions: which bodies are in scope, which heat sources can be displaced for a passing carbon claim, and which ownership arrangements need landlord or SPV consent [Salix].

It does not score the £510/tCO2e Grant Carbon Cost; that is the grant-size estimator. Eligibility says you can apply. The GCC says you can win.

What each outcome routes to

  • Eligible. Eligible lane → pre-feasibility checklist.
  • Conditional. Read the reason and the consent or fabric path back into scope.
  • Out of scope. Out-of-scope lane → PPA, Re:Fit, post-PSDS routes.

Source of rules

Encoded from the Salix Finance grant conditions and the DESNZ PSDS collection [DESNZ]. Refreshed when Phase 5 conditions are published.
