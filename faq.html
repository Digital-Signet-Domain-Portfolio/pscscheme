PSDS FAQ for schools

The questions a bursar asks first.

Is PSDS open right now?

No. Phase 4 closed November 2024, and at the June 2025 Spending Review the government confirmed no further PSDS investment beyond currently-awarded projects [Salix]. No Phase 5 has been announced. See Will there be a Phase 5?

Can independent schools apply?

No. PSDS is restricted to central government, the wider public sector, and state-funded educational institutions [DESNZ]. See ineligible lane.

Difference between PSDS and DfE R&R?

PSDS = DESNZ capital grant via Salix, scored on Grant Carbon Cost. R&R = £710m DfE allocation programme announced April 2026, scored on condition need [DfE]. See compare page.

Why was Phase 3c so oversubscribed?

First-come queue overwhelmed by pre-prepared bids after it opened on 10 October 2023. For Phase 4, Salix moved to targeted allocation by Grant Carbon Cost [Salix]. See Phase 3c page.

What is the £510/tCO2e ceiling?

Lifetime grant cost / lifetime tCO2e displaced. For Phase 4 the maximum Grant Carbon Cost was £510; Phase 3 used a tighter £325 [Salix]. See £510 ceiling page and grant estimator.

Who pays for the feasibility study?

The school or MAT internally. The Low Carbon Skills Fund that used to fund it is closed [DESNZ]. See feasibility page.

What is the delivery deadline?

Phase 3c: 31 March 2026. Phase 4: 31 March 2028 [DESNZ].

Can a PFI site apply?

Conditional: needs SPV consent in writing and the public-sector body as the named applicant.

Can I apply to both PSDS and DfE R&R?

Yes for the same estate but not for the same intervention.

What is the realistic timeline from kick-off to award?

Pre-feasibility 2-4 weeks, feasibility 6-10 weeks, portal window potentially short and oversubscribed (Phase 3c precedent), decision 8-12 weeks. Roughly 4-6 months from start to award assuming a window is open.
