DfE Renewal & Retrofit: the £710m programme

The Department for Education's Renewal & Retrofit programme is backed by £710 million of funding, running until 2029-30. It was set out in the February 2026 Education Estates Strategy and launched in April 2026 [DfE]. It is not open to bids: DfE selects schools and colleges on condition need [DfE].

What it funds

Projects that extend the life of school and college buildings which existing maintenance funding cannot cover but which do not need rebuilding, with the aim of making buildings climate-resilient and net zero-ready. Phase one concentrates on replacing end-of-life temporary modular buildings with permanent classrooms and installing solar PV where sites are suitable, including through power purchase agreements. Some sites also get an asset resilience and decarbonisation plan, estate management tools, behaviour change support and building controls optimisation [DfE].

Who is selected

DfE selects schools and colleges on condition need and contacts the responsible body; being contacted only means the school meets early suitability criteria. Phase one (2026 to 2027) covers Yorkshire and the Humber, the East Midlands and the South East. Regional delivery partners carry out surveys, feasibility, design and contractor management [DfE].

Difference vs PSDS

  • Decision route: DfE selection, no bids, vs a Salix portal application.
  • Test: condition need vs £/tCO2e Grant Carbon Cost.
  • Evidence: DfE's own condition data and partner surveys vs the applicant's feasibility study.
  • Scope: renewal-led vs decarbonisation-led.

Timeline

Funding runs to 2029-30, starting with the three phase one regions. If DfE contacts your responsible body, it will ask for information to assess suitability and confirmation that you can support delivery. If you are not contacted, no action is needed [DfE].

Using PSDS-era evidence

PSDS is closed to new applications. A heat decarbonisation plan or feasibility study prepared for PSDS is still useful if DfE or its delivery partner assesses your site, and for a CIF bid where the school is eligible.
